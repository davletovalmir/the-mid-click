How to middle click on a Mac

Middle click is a standard mouse button that macOS supports fully, but Apple's own input devices don't have it. What works depends on which device you're holding.

With a third-party mouse

Press the scroll wheel. macOS has supported the middle button since the first USB mice; nothing to configure. If a wheel press does nothing in a particular app, that app simply doesn't use middle click.

With a Magic Mouse

The Magic Mouse has one touch surface and reports only left and right clicks. There is no middle-click setting in System Settings. The same utilities that work for trackpads can map a gesture on it, but since its surface is small, most people pick a three-finger click rather than a tap.

With a trackpad

Built-in MacBook trackpads and the Magic Trackpad have no middle button either. Your options are a keyboard equivalent in apps that offer one, or a utility such as The Mid Click that turns a three-finger tap or click into a real middle-button event. The trackpad guide walks through both.

What "middle click" means to macOS

Internally it's mouse button number 2 (buttons count from zero), delivered to apps as otherMouseDown and otherMouseUp events. Any software that generates those events, or any hardware that reports a third button, gives you the real thing. The technical page follows the event from the trackpad's touch frames to the app.
