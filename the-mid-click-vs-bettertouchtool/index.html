The Mid Click vs BetterTouchTool

If you only want three-finger middle click on a Mac trackpad, The Mid Click is the simpler option. If you want extensive gesture customization and automation, BetterTouchTool is the more capable option. Both can produce a middle click from a three-finger gesture; they're built for different people.

Choose BetterTouchTool if

  • You already use it, or want window snapping, custom keyboard shortcuts, or per-app gesture sets. Adding middle click there is one more row in a list you already maintain.
  • You want the gesture to do different things in different apps.
  • You want more exotic triggers: corner clicks, force-touch levels, tip taps.

Choose The Mid Click if

  • Middle click is the only thing missing from your trackpad. You don't want to learn, configure, or back up a preferences tree to get it.
  • You want a tap that feels like a system gesture, with a test pad that shows the button macOS actually received.
  • You'd rather pay a dollar a year than a licence you'll renew for features you don't use.

Can you run both?

Yes, as long as only one of them is configured to handle the three-finger gesture. If both act, you'll get two middle clicks.

What they have in common

Both rely on the same macOS mechanism: an event tap that observes trackpad touches and sends a synthetic middle-button event, which is why both require Accessibility permission and why neither is sold on the Mac App Store. More on the permission.
