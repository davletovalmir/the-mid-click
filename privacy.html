Privacy Policy

Effective 20 September 2026

The Mid Click does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network connections.

What the app does

The Mid Click is a macOS utility that turns a three- or four-finger tap or click on the trackpad into a middle mouse click. It runs entirely on your Mac.

Accessibility permission

To work, the app asks for Accessibility access in System Settings. It uses this permission for exactly two things:

  • Reading how many fingers are touching the trackpad, and whether a mouse button was pressed, so it can recognise the gesture.
  • Sending a middle-click event to the app under the cursor.

The app never reads keystrokes, screen contents, window contents, or clipboard data. Touch and click information is processed in memory the instant it arrives and is not logged or saved.

Data collection

None. The app has no telemetry, crash reporting, or usage analytics of its own, and it does not communicate with any server. The only information stored on your Mac is your two settings (finger count and trigger mode) and your launch-at-login preference, kept in the app's own sandboxed preferences.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party code, SDKs, or advertising. If you install it from the Mac App Store, Apple's own privacy policy governs the store transaction and any diagnostics you have chosen to share with Apple.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
