Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

The Mid Click does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts and no analytics.

What the app does

The Mid Click is a macOS utility that turns a three- or four-finger tap or click on the trackpad into a middle mouse click. It runs entirely on your Mac.

Accessibility permission

To work, the app asks for Accessibility access in System Settings. It uses this permission for exactly two things:

  • Reading how many fingers are touching the trackpad, and whether a mouse button was pressed, so it can recognise the gesture.
  • Sending a middle-click event to the app under the cursor.

The app never reads keystrokes, screen contents, window contents, or clipboard data. Touch and click information is processed in memory the instant it arrives and is not logged or saved.

Network use

The app makes two kinds of network request.

  • License checks. When you enter a license key, and about once a week afterwards, it sends that key and a device name (your Mac's name by default) to Lemon Squeezy, the payment provider, to confirm the key is valid.
  • Update checks. About once a day it downloads a small file from themidclick.com listing the latest version, and installs a new version in the background if there is one. The request carries no identifier and no information about you or your Mac.

Nothing else is sent, ever.

Purchases happen on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page in your browser, under Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy. The app does not see your payment details.

Data stored on your Mac

Your two settings (finger count and trigger mode), the launch-at-login preference, the date your trial started, and your license key if you entered one. They live in the app's own preferences inside its sandbox container.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party code, SDKs, or advertising.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
